Innovation, Sustainability & Community Empowerment.

Customised Solutions for Every Mining & Engineering Challenge!

Bahlaping Mash was established in 2016 with a clear purpose — to challenge the norms of a traditionally male-dominated industry and deliver exceptional mining, electrical, and civil engineering services across Southern Africa. As a BBBEE Level 1 black woman-owned enterprise, we bring not only technical excellence but also a deep commitment to transformation, community upliftment, and sustainable industry practices. We are registered as both an ARIBA and COUPA supplier and are trusted by De Beers Group, Kumba Iron Ore, and Anglo American.
