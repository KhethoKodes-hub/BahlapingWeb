About Bahlaping Mash

A BBBEE Level 1 Black Woman-Owned Company Based in Limpopo, South Africa.

Delivering High-Performance Mining, Engineering & Construction Services Across Southern Africa!

Bahlaping Mash is a distinguished black woman-owned company headquartered in Mapela district in Mokopane town, Limpopo province, South Africa. Established in 2016, this BBBEE Level 1 company saw a distinctive opportunity to challenge the norms of the traditionally male-dominated sectors of mining and engineering.

With a vision to diversify and expand, Bahlaping Mash has since been on a mission to provide a spectrum of services encompassing mining, electrical, and mechanical engineering, aiming to bridge the gender disparity in the industry. We are registered as both an ARIBA and COUPA supplier, working alongside industry giants such as De Beers Group, Kumba Iron Ore, and Anglo American.

  • BBBEE Level 1 — Committed to Transformation & Empowerment
  • Highly Experienced Team Across Mining, Electrical & Civil Disciplines
  • Registered ARIBA & COUPA Supplier — Trusted by Major Mining Groups
  • Community-Focused, Sustainable, and Innovation-Driven Solutions
