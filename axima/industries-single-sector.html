Overview

Bahlaping Mash specialises in chrome mining and export within the Southern African market, operating across Zimbabwe's chrome ore-rich areas between Lalapanzi, Mvurwi, and Mapinga. As a fully integrated chrome operator, we manage the entire value chain — from ore extraction and processing through to export logistics and delivery to end markets in Asia.
Our Operations & Capacity

Our chrome operations are anchored by our expertise in the design, installation, and operation of chrome spiral wash plants — the core processing technology used to separate and concentrate chrome ore. Our engineering team handles the full project lifecycle from wash plant design and civil foundation work through to installation, commissioning, and ongoing operations management. This integrated capability enables us to optimise recovery rates and consistently produce export-grade chrome concentrates that meet the specifications demanded by Asian steel mills and ferrochrome producers.

Wash Plant Design & Installation

We specialise in the design and installation of chrome spiral wash plants tailored to site-specific ore grades and volumes. Our plants are engineered for maximum chrome recovery, minimal water consumption, and ease of operation in remote locations across Zimbabwe and South Africa.

Export & Logistics

We manage the full export chain — from chrome concentrate stockpiling at our Zimbabwe operations through to road and rail transport, port logistics, and final delivery to customers in Asian markets including China. Our logistics network ensures reliable, on-schedule shipments.

Where does Bahlaping Mash operate its chrome mining?

Our chrome mining and processing operations are centred in Zimbabwe's chrome ore-rich belt between Lalapanzi, Mvurwi, and Mapinga. We also operate chrome-related activities in South Africa, with concentrate export flowing to Asian markets. Our established presence in these regions gives us a competitive advantage in ore access, community relationships, and regulatory compliance.

What is a chrome spiral wash plant and how does it work?

A chrome spiral wash plant is a gravity-based mineral processing system that separates chrome ore from gangue material using spirally shaped channels and water flow. As slurried ore travels down the spiral, denser chrome particles concentrate on the inner edge while lighter waste material moves to the outer edge and is discharged. Bahlaping Mash designs, installs, and operates these plants to maximise chrome concentrate recovery from our mining areas.

Which markets does Bahlaping Mash export chrome concentrate to?

Bahlaping Mash produces and exports chrome concentrates primarily to Asian markets, with China being the principal destination. Asian steel mills and ferrochrome smelters represent the world's largest consumers of chrome ore, and our export-grade concentrates are positioned to meet their quality and volume requirements. We manage all logistics from our Zimbabwe and South Africa operations through to port loading and final delivery.
