# DAILY STANDUP TEMPLATE & TEAM COMMUNICATION GUIDE
**For:** Bahlaping Mash Website 2.0 Project  
**Frequency:** Daily (9:00 AM or project start time)  
**Duration:** 10-15 minutes

---

## 📋 DAILY STANDUP FORMAT

### Standard 3-Question Format

Each team member answers:

1. **✅ What did I complete yesterday?**
   - Be specific (not "I worked on forms", but "I set up contact form validation")
   - Include any blockers you resolved

2. **🎯 What am I working on today?**
   - 2-3 specific tasks
   - Expected completion time
   - Any dependencies

3. **⚠️ Do I have any blockers?**
   - Things slowing me down
   - Things I need help with
   - Missing information or assets
   - Decisions needed from client

## 🚨 ESCALATION PROTOCOL

### Level 1: Team Member
- Document the issue clearly
- Attempt basic troubleshooting
- Post in project Slack
- Expected response: 1 hour

### Level 2: Project Lead
- Email project lead with details
- Include screenshots/error messages
- Suggest potential solutions
- Expected response: 2 hours

### Level 3: Client (Sharon)
- Project lead contacts Sharon
- Only for client-approval decisions
- Urgent issues only
- Expected response: 4-24 hours

### Level 4: Emergency (Launch Day)
- Direct call to all team leads
- Video call war room if needed
- All hands on deck
- Expected resolution: Immediate

## 🗣️ COMMUNICATION ETIQUETTE

### Slack/Teams Guidelines
- ✅ Use threads for long conversations
- ✅ Use emojis for clarity (✅ done, 🔄 waiting, ⚠️ blocked)
- ✅ @ mention for urgent items
- ✅ Give context (include links, screenshots)
- ❌ Don't use for urgent urgent things (call instead)

### Email Guidelines
- ✅ Clear subject line with project name
- ✅ Use template format (from CLIENT_COMMUNICATION.md)
- ✅ Include stakeholders appropriately
- ✅ Use CC for visibility, not responsibility
- ✅ Send weekly updates Friday 5 PM
- ❌ Don't email for urgent items (call first)

### Meeting Guidelines
- ✅ Start/end on time
- ✅ Stick to 10-minute standup
- ✅ Use video (cameras on)
- ✅ Share screen when discussing code
- ✅ Record for those who can't attend
- ❌ Don't multitask during meetings

## ✨ SUCCESS INDICATORS

### Green Light (Keep Going!)
- ✅ All daily standups attended
- ✅ No critical blockers
- ✅ Tasks completing on schedule
- ✅ Client feedback positive
- ✅ Quality metrics on target

### Yellow Light (Pay Attention!)
- 🟡 One team member frequently blocked
- 🟡 Assets late from client
- 🟡 Performance targets at risk
- 🟡 Testing finding significant issues
- 🟡 Scope creep starting

### Red Light (Emergency!)
- 🔴 Major blocker stopping all work
- 🔴 Client requesting significant changes
- 🔴 Technical issue requiring rework
- 🔴 Team member unable to contribute
- 🔴 Launch date at risk

**Action:** Yellow = team discussion, Red = escalate immediately
